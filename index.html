Questions practices actually ask

We already have a recall system.

Most practices do — and most of those systems send the same reminder that was written years ago, to everyone, without anyone opening the overdue list. We keep your system and work the list it produces: three groups, three different messages, 21 days of follow-through. If the system is fine and the copy is fine, you will not need us, and the free report will show that.

Do we have to hand over patient information?

No. The office sends from its own phone, PMS or mail merge. We receive counts (how many were contacted, replied, booked, attended) and production totals — never a name, a birth date or an insurance ID.

What if it doesn’t work?

If we completed the three planned sends and fewer than five patients rebooked, your next month of Recall Care is free. We will not promise a number of patients: it depends on your list, your front desk and your schedule. We will show you the counts at every step so you can decide to stop.

What do you need from us?

Three counts on day 0, about 30 minutes a week to approve and send, and the tracker numbers at the end of each week. No PMS integration, no install, no IT ticket.

Why English and Korean?

In Flushing the same practice has patients who answer a Korean text and patients who ignore anything that is not English. We write both and your front desk picks per patient — it is the cheapest response-rate improvement available.

How do we pay, and is there a contract?

We send a PayPal invoice, and you can pay it by card or PayPal balance, or through paypal.me/nydds. If you would rather not use PayPal, Zelle is listed on the same invoice, with no fee. No contract: the sprint is a fixed $249, and the monthly care plan can be stopped with one email.

Who is behind this?

Recall Rescue Team — a dental operations team based in Flushing, Queens, working only on recall and no-show recovery: not websites, not marketing retainers. We are independent: not affiliated with any PMS vendor, and not connected to any practice we might be compared with.
